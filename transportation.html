Arriving in Taniti

All transit onto the island originates by sea or air. Review your available arrival points below:

  • Air Travel: Taniti is currently served by a small airport that can accommodate small jets and propeller planes. Taniti is in the process of expanding the airport so larger jets will be able to land on the island within the next few years.
  • Cruise Ships: Some visitors choose to arrive via sea route on a small cruise ship. These vessels dock weekly at Yellow Leaf Bay for a single night.

Ground Transportation

  • Buses: Public buses serve Taniti City and run from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island.
  • Taxis & Vehicle Rentals: Taxis are available in Taniti City, and rental cars can be rented from a local rental agency near the airport.
  • Bicycle Rentals: Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors. Please note that helmets are strictly required by law for all cyclists.
  • Walking & Pedestrian Areas: Taniti City features flat terrain and is highly walkable. Many tourists stay in the area surrounding Merriton Landing as this area is easy to explore on foot.
